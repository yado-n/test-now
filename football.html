Description

A burger is a popular dish made with a cooked beef patty placed inside a sliced bun.

It is usually served with cheese, lettuce, tomato, and sauce to make a delicious and satisfying meal.

Ingredients

  • Burger buns
  • Ground beef
  • Cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Salt and pepper

Steps to prepare burger

  1. Season the ground beef with salt and pepper.
  2. Shape the ground beef into burger patties.
  3. Heat a pan and cook the patties on both sides.
  4. Place cheese on the patties and allow it to melt.
  5. Cut the burger buns and prepare the lettuce, tomato, and onion.
  6. Place the cooked patty and vegetables inside the buns.
  7. Add your favorite sauce and serve the burger.
